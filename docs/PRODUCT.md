# Tashi Joyería
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript landing. Local development only; no framework or SaaS dependency.

## Users
People exploring Tashi's jewelry and seeking a conversation about a personal piece. Primary conversion confirmed by the owner of this task: request a quote through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Tashi, showcase real jewelry, explain personalization and invite a quotation.

## Positioning
Tashi's website states that Emily Sibaja founded the brand to create delicate, elegant personalized jewelry in 18K gold with precious and semiprecious stones. Instagram states “Cada joya, un legado”. The website lists Barranquilla, Colombia, and national/international shipping.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Tashi's existing logo and colors. Build an original composition, not a copy of another website. The catalog photograph of the Dual Gems white-gold emerald ring is approved for the hero. The primary action is quotation via WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Work exclusively on local files. Never publish to, sync with, or modify the existing Tashi website. No invented prices, testimonials, certifications, guarantees, response times or discounts. Photograph-led motion is not a true 360-degree model. Payment and order fulfillment are outside this landing's scope.

## Product Principles
- Let actual jewelry lead the experience.
- Make a quotation conversation easy to start.
- Distinguish real business facts from visual storytelling.
- Keep motion gentle, optional, and responsive.
